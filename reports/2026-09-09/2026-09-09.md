Reason: `target reached (euphoric)`, fired automatically by `reprice.py` under exit rule 4.

This needs explaining, because it looks like a win and is not one. Nutrien scored **2.5** on real
fundamentals — revenue $27.29B +9.1%, EPS $4.93 **+78.3%**, net income +74.6%, record potash volumes,
the largest potash producer in the world. But at $80.68 it traded **5.3% above its $76.43 average
target**, which is the definition of the `euphoric` overlay, which caps it at **WATCH — do not chase**.
No position was ever taken.

The ledger then closed it the same session, because exit rule 4 fires when `last_price >= target` **and**
the overlay is euphoric — and a euphoric name is *by definition* already above its average target. **Any
euphoric WATCH pick auto-closes on the day it is added.** Counting that as a closed trade would have
published a **100% win rate on a trade that was never entered**, which is the exact failure `06_track.md`
records against FLR on 2026-09-06. It is flagged `bookkeeping: true` and excluded from the record, which
therefore still reads **0 closed, win rate `null`** — not 0%, and not 100%.
