# Scoring Rubric — Momentum Verification Analysis

The framework scores three equally-weighted parts (each 0 / 0.5 / 1.0) for a total
out of **3.0**, then layers a non-scored **technical overlay** on top. The verdict is
derived from how the fundamental score sits *relative to* the technical picture.

> Principle: **Identify with Momentum, Verify with Fundamentals.** Momentum surfaces
> the candidate; the three parts decide whether the move is *earned*. Divergence
> between the two is where the actionable signal lives.

---

## Part 1 — Quality Check (Margins & Profitability)

**Question:** Is the price move supported by margin trends?

| Score | Criteria |
|------|----------|
| **1.0** | Margins **clearly expanding** off a healthy base; profitability strong and improving (e.g. operating margin up several hundred bps over 1–2 years, strong FCF). Pricing power evident. |
| **0.5** | Margins **expanding but thin / early-cycle**, OR healthy but flat. Direction is right but the base is low or the gain is volume-driven rather than structural. |
| **0.0** | Margins **contracting**; profitability deteriorating or negative with no clear path up. |

Look at: gross & operating margin trend (multi-period), net margin, EPS trajectory,
FCF, and *why* margins are moving (mix/pricing vs. cost vs. volume).

---

## Part 2 — Growth Check (Divisional / Segment Analysis)

**Question:** Which division is driving growth, and is it sustainable?

| Score | Criteria |
|------|----------|
| **1.0** | Growth is **price/mix-driven or secular** (ARPU, premium tier, new structural demand) — generally *more* durable. Healthy double-digit growth with a clear, repeatable engine. |
| **0.5** | Growth is **volume-driven / cyclical**, decelerating but still solid, or transitioning. Real demand (e.g. strong book-to-bill, backlog) but exposed to the cycle. |
| **0.0** | Growth **stalling, one-off, or driven by an unsustainable factor**. |

Look at: segment revenue mix and growth rates, volume vs. price contribution,
order signals (book-to-bill, backlog), and management's guided growth engines.

> Heuristic: **price/mix-led growth usually scores higher than volume-led**, because
> it implies pricing power and is less cycle-dependent.

---

## Part 3 — Peer Check (Relative Strength)

**Question:** Is the company leading or lagging its peer group — especially in the
specific trend/catalyst it is being bought for?

| Score | Criteria |
|------|----------|
| **1.0** | Clear **leader / share gainer**; sets price; pure-play exposure to the driving trend. |
| **0.5** | **In-line / diversified beneficiary** — a follower that participates but doesn't lead; or has lost dominance while keeping a moat (brand, installed base). |
| **0.0** | **Laggard / losing share**; a price-taker with only peripheral exposure to the trend. |

Look at: market-share ranking, who holds pricing power, and—if the user named a
trend (AI, MLCC, GLP-1, robotaxi, etc.)—whether this company is the *right horse* or
just *in the race*. A stock can rally on a narrative while scoring low here; flag that
gap explicitly.

---

## Technical Overlay (not scored)

Characterize the chart and the Street, then carry it into the verdict:

| State | Markers |
|------|---------|
| **Bullish (aligned)** | Uptrend intact, above key MAs, healthy RSI (40–65). |
| **Oversold / washed-out** | Falling channel, broken support, RSI < 30, well off highs. Potential *entry on stabilization*. |
| **Euphoric / overbought** | Near-vertical advance, at/near 52-wk high, RSI > 70–80, large 1-yr gain, Street targets *below* price. Late-stage. |
| **Bearish (aligned)** | Downtrend, sell signals, deteriorating fundamentals confirm. |

Also record analyst consensus rating + price target vs. current price (the implied
up/downside is a useful reality check on the crowd).

---

## Verdict Logic (fundamentals × technicals)

| Fundamental score | Technical state | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| High (≥2.0) | Oversold / bearish | **Accumulate on weakness** — don't catch a falling knife; wait for stabilization near support. |
| High (≥2.0) | Bullish / aligned | **Buy / Add** — alignment; momentum is earned. |
| Low–mid (≤1.5) | Euphoric / overbought | **Trim / Take profits — don't chase** — momentum has run ahead of fundamentals. |
| Low–mid (≤1.5) | Bearish | **Avoid / Sell** — nothing supports the name. |
| Mid (≈2.0) | Mixed | **Watch / position-size carefully** — wait for confirmation. |

Always state an **Accumulate/Trim zone**, a **stop**, and a **target** (anchor the
target to analyst consensus or a defensible level), plus guidance for an existing
position vs. a new one.

> The two most useful, repeatable setups are the **divergences**: strong
> fundamentals + bearish technicals (buy weakness), and weak/neutral fundamentals +
> euphoric technicals (sell strength). Name the divergence explicitly in the report.
