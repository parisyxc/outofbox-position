# Sources Checklist — what to gather before scoring

Research **first**, score **second**. Never put a number in the report you didn't
find in a source. Always search for the *most recent* reported period (the framework
is point-in-time; date the report).

## 1. Latest earnings & financials  → feeds Part 1 (Quality) + earnings table
- [ ] Most recent quarter: revenue (with YoY and QoQ), and the period reported.
- [ ] Gross margin, operating margin, net margin — and the **trend** vs. prior quarters/year.
- [ ] EPS (GAAP and/or adjusted); whether it beat/missed.
- [ ] Free cash flow.
- [ ] Forward **guidance** (next quarter and/or full year): revenue and margin.
- [ ] *Why* margins moved (pricing/mix vs. cost vs. volume).

Good sources: company press release / 8-K, earnings-call transcript (Motley Fool,
Investing.com), StockTitan, the IR site, Yahoo/Stockanalysis for the summary table.

## 2. Growth composition  → feeds Part 2 (Growth)
- [ ] Segment / divisional revenue and growth rates — which division leads.
- [ ] Volume vs. price/mix contribution to growth.
- [ ] Order signals where relevant: **book-to-bill**, **backlog** (months / trend).
- [ ] Management's named growth engines / end-markets.

## 3. Peer group & relative position  → feeds Part 3 (Peer)
- [ ] Who are the 3–5 closest peers; market-share ranking.
- [ ] Who holds **pricing power** in the category.
- [ ] If the user named a **trend/catalyst** (AI, MLCC, GLP-1, EV, robotaxi…):
      is this company a **leader / pure-play** or a **follower / peripheral** beneficiary?
      Quantify exposure (e.g. "segment X is only ~Y% of revenue").
- [ ] Relative stock performance vs. peers / sector.

Good sources: industry/market-research notes, trade press, sell-side summaries,
the trend's own supply-chain coverage.

## 4. Technicals & the Street  → feeds the Technical Overlay + zones
- [ ] Current price; 52-week high/low; 1-year (or YTD) return.
- [ ] RSI (14-day) and trend/channel; key support & resistance levels.
- [ ] Analyst **consensus rating** and **price target** (and implied up/downside).
- [ ] Any very recent catalyst (guidance, deal, product) moving the chart.

Good sources: MarketBeat, Stockanalysis, Investing.com, TradingView technicals,
broker target changes.

## Quality bar
- Prefer primary (company filings/transcripts) and reputable financial sources.
- Cross-check the 2–3 headline numbers (revenue, margin, EPS, price, target) across
  at least two sources before locking them in.
- Record every URL used → the report ends with a **Sources** list.
- If a figure can't be verified, say so in the report rather than guessing.
